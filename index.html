Create an SVG card

Enter a public repository and choose a palette. Download a card with its description, language, activity and available release metadata.

Accepts owner/repository or an HTTPS github.com repository URL.

Metadata is fetched directly from api.github.com. No tokens, cookies, or private repositories are used.

Sample data

Save the SVG in your repository as assets/repository-card.svg, then paste the Markdown into your README.

Cards are snapshots. Regenerate to refresh the data. “Latest workflow” describes one workflow run, not the status of every check. Unavailable metadata is labelled explicitly.
